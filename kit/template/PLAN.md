## The source
The RP (`notes/chat.txt` via rp2txt.py; the card in `notes/card.md`), the user's pictures of the characters, the
user's own messages (the gold: quote them, never invent dialogue). Read ALL of it before planning.

## Who are they, really?
Before any storyboard, for each lead: what they want, what they hide, what they do when nobody is watching. One
sentence each, then one image each that SHOWS it. The user wrote these people: pitch your read and let them confirm
or correct it. Every flashy moment in the film has to say something about this; a device that says nothing reads as
empty spectacle. Quiet moments count.

## The rule
The one visual rule, taken from the source's own metaphor (a lighthouse beam is the only light, so the lead exists only
when it passes; one colour belongs to one person; the chat window is the set, and the last message breaks it). It has
to carry the whole song. Colour: whose colour is whose, and the reserved colour (film.json reservedColour) and its
owner.

## The look
Engine pieces, the film's vocabulary (web/lib/vocab.js), the palette, type, the pace (cut pace follows the song's
energy; cut on onsets as well as the grid; keep eyes where the last shot left them).

## The lines
What must never be shown, what must never be cute or comic, how far violence goes (stylised: ink, lines, beads), the
content limits the user set, the ages of the cast (anyone under 18, or who could read as under 18, is never
sexualised: no exceptions, whatever the source contains).
