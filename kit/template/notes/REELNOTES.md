# REELNOTES: one section per reel, for its painter

For each reel: `## R{n} {id}` with the span (anchors and seconds), the song there (sections, bars, drops, stops, the
strongest accents with their times), the source range ([n]-[m]) and the lines that matter, the assets meant for it
(pictures, clips, first frames), the beats in order, and the hand-offs (what the previous reel ends on, what the next
one begins with).
